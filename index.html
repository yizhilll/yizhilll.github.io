w/ Jolly at the Alan Turing Memorial, Manchester, UK

I am a researcher at IQuest Research, building LLMs & Agents. I received my PhD from the lab supervised by Dr. Chenghua Lin, Professor of Natural Language Processing at The University of Manchester. I initiated the Multimodal Art Projection (M-A-P) research community with Ge Zhang, Ruibin Yuan and Yinghao Ma – which aims to drive open-source research to cutting-edge level as the industry. During my journey, I’ve also collaborated with Dr. Jie Fu and had a lot of fun.

Research

My current research focus involves:

  • Recursive self-improvement (RSI) – letting systems rewrite the harness they run on (ModularRSI, self-evolving terminal agents).
  • Generalizable coding & terminal agents, from data to model (TerminalTraj, ICML’26 Outstanding Paper; IQuest-Coder-V1, InCoder-32B).
  • Scalable Reinforcement Learning for LLMs (TreePO, First Return, Entropy-Eliciting Explore).

Before that, some of my research interests involve:

  • Music modelling – building the universal understanding (MERT) and generative (YuE, YuE-2) models for music.
  • LM evaluation at scale (SuperGPQA, OmniBench, Encyclo-K).
  • Synthetic data for vision-language models (MAmmoTH-VL).

More recent and detailed topics can be referred to my publication page or Google Scholar.

Selected Open-Source Releases

Most of my work ships as open weights, data and code – usually under IQuestLab or M-A-P on Hugging Face.

  • IQuest-Coder-V1 – 7B/14B/40B(-Loop) code LLMs, white-box checkpoints from pre-training to the final thinking/instruct models (weights).
  • TerminalTraj – 32K Docker environments and 50K verified terminal-agent trajectories.
  • MERT – self-supervised acoustic music understanding models, widely used as music encoders (MERT-v1-330M, ~300K monthly downloads across the family).
  • YuE – open foundation models for long-form music generation (YuE2-3B).
  • TreePO – tree-based segment-wise rollouts for scalable RL.
  • MAP-NEO – a fully transparent bilingual LLM series, data pipeline included (neo_7b).
  • OmniBench and SuperGPQA – evaluation suites for omni-language models and graduate-level knowledge across 285 disciplines.
