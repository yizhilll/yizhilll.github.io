w/ Jolly at the Alan Turing Memorial, Manchester, UK

I am a researcher at IQuest Research, UbiQuant, building Coding LLMs & Agents. I received my PhD from the lab supervised by Dr. Chenghua Lin, Professor of Natural Language Processing at The University of Manchester. I initiated the Multimodal Art Projection (M-A-P) research community with Ge Zhang, Ruibin Yuan and Yinghao Ma – which aims to drive open-source research to cutting-edge level as the industry. During my journey, I’ve also collaborated with Dr. Jie Fu and had a lot of fun.

Research

My current research focus involves:

  • Recursive self-improvement (RSI) – letting systems rewrite the harness they run on (ModularRSI, self-evolving terminal agents).
  • Generalizable coding & terminal agents, from data to model (IQuest-Coder-V1, InCoder-32B, TerminalTraj @ ICML’26).
  • Scalable Reinforcement Learning for LLMs (TreePO, First Return, Entropy-Eliciting Explore).

Before that, some of my research interests involve:

  • LM evaluation at scale (SuperGPQA, OmniBench, Encyclo-K).
  • Synthetic data for vision-language models (MAmmoTH-VL).
  • Music modelling – building the universal understanding (MERT) and generative (YuE) models for music.

More recent and detailed topics can be referred to my publication page or Google Scholar.

Past Experience

  • Interned at J.P. Morgan Artificial Intelligence Research in 2024 summer.
